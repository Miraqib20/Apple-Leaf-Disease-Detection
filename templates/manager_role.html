🍂 Seasonal Tips

Spring

Apply copper-based fungicide before bud break to prevent scab

Summer

Monitor for fire blight — prune infected shoots 30cm below visible damage

Autumn

Remove all fallen leaves — they harbour overwintering fungal spores

Winter

Apply dormant oil spray to kill overwintering mites and scale insects

4. Pruning & Tree Management

Guides on when and how to prune, plus activity logging saved to database.

📅

When to Prune

  • Late winter (Feb–Mar) before bud break
  • Immediately after detecting fire blight
  • After harvest to shape the canopy
✂️

How to Prune

  • Cut at 45° angle just above a bud
  • Remove crossing or rubbing branches first
  • Disinfect tools between each tree with 70% alcohol
🎯

Goals

  • Open canopy for sunlight penetration
  • Improve air circulation to reduce fungal risk
  • Remove dead, diseased, or damaged wood
